The Order previews API has seven endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/orders/preview`. You start a preview with the products, the customer and the delivery type, read the priced result, apply a coupon or a gift card, and place the order from the preview. Starting a preview places no order.

<Warning>
  **[Place the order from a preview](/api-reference/order-previews/place-the-order-from-a-preview) creates a real order.**
  It closes the preview, and every later call on it, including a second confirm, is rejected with `410`.
  A preview also ends on its own: it expires 30 minutes after it was started or after the last time you
  applied or removed a coupon or a gift card. Start a new preview when you need one again.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication (New)](/authentication), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy) if your store
signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The path takes the `session_id` of the preview.** [Start an order preview](/api-reference/order-previews/start-an-order-preview) returns it as
  `order.session.session_id`, a value such as `PRV-3f2a9c41b7d80e65`. Every other call takes it as
  `preview_session_id` in the path. An unknown value returns `404` with the code `not_found`.
- **A preview lasts 30 minutes from its last change.** `order.session.expires_at` is 30 minutes after
  `created_at`. Each time you apply or remove a coupon or a gift card, `expires_at` moves to 30 minutes after that
  call. [Retrieve an order preview](/api-reference/order-previews/retrieve-an-order-preview) does not move it.
- **You send only `product_id` and `quantity` for each product.** The store reads the prices, taxes and
  shipping cost itself, so a `price` or `product_name` in a product line is rejected with `400` and the reason
  `unknown_field`. A body field the call does not accept, such as `coupon_code`, is rejected the same way: apply a
  coupon with [Apply a coupon to a preview](/api-reference/order-previews/apply-a-coupon-to-a-preview).
- **Send `customer_id`, or send a billing address.** With `customer_id` alone, the preview uses the customer's
  saved addresses. Without `customer_id` and without `billing_address`, the call is rejected with `400`, and so is a
  preview that has a product to ship and no shipping address.
- **`delivery_type` is `delivery`, `others_shipping`, `shipping` or `store_pickup`.** It is `shipping` when you leave it
  out. With `store_pickup`, `shipping.shipping_cost` is `0` and the grand total is lower by that amount.
- **A preview takes one coupon and one gift card at a time.** A second coupon, even the same code, is rejected with
  `409` and the code `coupon_already_applied`, and a second gift card with `gift_card_already_applied`. Remove the
  applied one first with [Remove the coupon from a preview](/api-reference/order-previews/remove-the-coupon-from-a-preview) or
  [Remove the gift card from a preview](/api-reference/order-previews/remove-the-gift-card-from-a-preview).
- **A coupon only works if its discount profile applies to every customer and every product.** Create the profile with
  [Create a discount profile](/api-reference/discount-profiles/create-a-discount-profile) and send `is_applied_to_all_customers` `true`,
  `is_applied_to_all_products` `true` and `category` `[]`. Without them, applying the code is rejected with `422` and
  the message `Invalid Code`. The code you send is the profile's `default_coupon_code`.
- **A gift card pays only for the customer whose email address it holds.** The card's `email` must equal the email of
  the customer record you previewed; the billing address's email does not count. Find a customer's cards with
  [List gift cards](/api-reference/gift-cards/list-gift-cards) and its `email` filter, then use the card in
  [Apply a gift card to a preview](/api-reference/order-previews/apply-a-gift-card-to-a-preview). Applying a gift card lowers `due` and leaves
  `grand_total` as it was, and the preview spends nothing from the card.
