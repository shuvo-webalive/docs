The SEO configuration API has fifteen endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/settings/seo_configuration`.

<Warning>
  **A `PUT` resets every field you leave out of a section you send.** This applies on
  `/seo_configuration` and on `/seo_configuration/{section}`. A section you leave out keeps its
  values, but inside a section you send, each missing field goes back to its default: sending only
  `regenerate_url_on_item_update` in `url_settings` resets a stored `true` in
  `auto_redirect_on_url_change` to `false`. To change some fields and keep the rest, use `PATCH`,
  or send each section whole.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **SEO configuration has three settings sections and two collections.** `global_store_settings`,
  `custom_code` and `url_settings` are settings: read or write all three through `/seo_configuration`,
  or one through `/seo_configuration/{section}`. `document_root` (the uploaded files) and
  `redirects` are collections that you manage through their own paths. `group_meta` marks both
  collections `writable: false`, even though you can add and delete their rows.
- **Settings writes are wrapped, and the wrapper depends on the path.** On `/seo_configuration`, wrap
  the fields in `seo_configuration` and then in the section name. On `/seo_configuration/{section}`,
  wrap them in the section's own name, such as `url_settings`: a body that is not wrapped is rejected
  with `400 invalid_request`. Redirect writes need no wrapper: send `source_url` and
  `destination_url` as the body itself.
- **`custom_code` is code the storefront serves on every page.** `header.code` is raw HTML or
  JavaScript added to `<head>`, and `footer.code` is added before `</body>`. `field_metadata` lists
  each `code` as requiring its block's `enabled` (`header.enabled` or `footer.enabled`).
- **A collection listing returns at most 100 rows per call.** The default and the maximum `limit` are
  `100`: a larger value is capped, and `pagination.limit` shows `100`. Send a higher `offset` to get
  the next rows while `pagination.has_next` is `true`. `pagination` has five keys: `total`, `limit`,
  `offset`, `has_previous` and `has_next`.
- **Writes are limited in a ten-second window.** The store accepts fewer writes than reads in each
  fixed ten-second window, and a write over the limit is rejected with a `Retry-After` header. A retry
  sent before then counts against the same limit, so wait the time `Retry-After` gives before you
  write again.
