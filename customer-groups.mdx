The Customer groups API has nine endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/customer_groups`.

<Warning>
  **Replacing a group's members removes every customer you leave out of the list.**
  [Replace the members of a customer group](/api-reference/customer-groups/replace-the-members-of-a-customer-group) makes the list you send the
  whole membership, and `{"customers": []}` empties the group. To add one customer and keep the
  others, use [Add a customer to a customer group](/api-reference/customer-groups/add-a-customer-to-a-customer-group).
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Wrap a group in `customer_group` when you create or update it.** Send
  `{"customer_group": {"name": "Wholesale Partners"}}`. A body without the wrapper is rejected
  with `400` and the message `customer_group is required`. Replacing the members and adding a
  customer take bodies without that wrapper: `{"customers": [...]}` and
  `{"customer": ..., "group": ...}`.
- **An update changes only the fields you send, inside nested settings too.**
  `{"customer_group": {"tax_configuration": {"tax_exempt": true}}}` changes `tax_exempt` and keeps
  the other five tax settings. There is no replace: `PUT` on a group is rejected with `405`.
- **Members are customers, identified by their `customer_id`.** The member calls take the customer's
  `customer_id`, as a number or a string, not its `internal_id`. A customer's groups are listed
  under `customer_groups` when you [retrieve the customer](/api-reference/customers/retrieve-a-customer).
- **A listing returns 20 groups per call unless you send `limit`.** `limit` takes `1` to `100`. To get
  more, send a higher `offset`, or `page`, while `pagination.has_next` is `true`. To sort by date,
  send `sort=created` or `sort=updated`: `created_at` and `updated_at` are rejected with `400`.
- **There is no `HEAD` call.** `HEAD` returns `404` on every customer group path, even though the
  `Allow` header of an `OPTIONS` request lists it on the listing, count and group paths. Use `GET`
  instead.
- **Groups already in the store can hold values a write rejects.** A stored group can have a `status`
  other than `A` or `I`, a `default_tax_code` a create rejects, or a `name` with spaces at either end.
  Such a group matches neither `status=A` nor `status=I`, so those two counts can add up to less
  than the total.
