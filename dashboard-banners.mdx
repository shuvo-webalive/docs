The Dashboard banners API has six endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Five paths start with `/api/v4/admin/dashboard/banners`, and the feed is at `/api/v4/admin/dashboard/content_info`.

<Warning>
  **Banners are store-wide: a banner you create, change or delete shows on the dashboard of every admin.**
  A created banner appears in [Get the dashboard feed](/api-reference/dashboard-banners/get-the-dashboard-feed) at once while `active` is `true`,
  and it stays until you delete it with [Delete a dashboard banner](/api-reference/dashboard-banners/delete-a-dashboard-banner). Creating, moving
  or deleting a banner also changes the `position` of the banners after it. The store already has banners of its own,
  so list them with [List dashboard banners](/api-reference/dashboard-banners/list-dashboard-banners) before you write, and change only banners you created.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication (New)](/authentication), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy) if your store
signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Send the fields of every write as they are, with no wrapper.** Create and update take the banner's fields at
  the top level of the body, and responses return the banner the same way. A field a banner does not have is
  rejected with `400` and the reason `unknown_field`, and one `400` lists every field that failed.
- **Pass the banner's `key` as `banner_key`.** The `key` is a short name such as `spring_sale`: lowercase
  letters, digits and `_`, starting with a letter, up to 64 characters, and unique. You choose it when you
  create the banner, and you cannot change it afterwards. A `key` that matches no banner is rejected with `404`
  and the reason `not_found`.
- **`learn_more` is required, and it takes one of two forms.** `{"action": "open_link", "label": "...",
  "url": "https://..."}` opens a link. `{"action": "open_modal", "label": "...", "title": "...",
  "subtitle": "...", "steps": [{"title": "...", "description": "..."}]}` opens a window with at least
  one step. A `url` must start with `https`. `try_it` is optional: `{"label": "...", "url": "..."}` adds a
  second button, and `null` means none.
- **`position` counts from `1` over every banner, hidden ones included.** Without `position`, a new banner goes
  last. With one, from `1` to one more than the number of banners, the banners from that place move down by one.
  Deleting a banner moves the banners after it back up, so the positions always run `1`, `2`, `3` and on.
- **An update changes only what you send, and replaces `learn_more` and `try_it` whole.** An `open_modal`
  `learn_more` sent over an `open_link` one drops the `url`. `"try_it": null` removes the second button. `key` cannot
  be sent, and an empty body is rejected with `400`. `icon` is saved as you send it and is not checked
  against a list.
- **A banner with `active` `false` stays in the list but leaves the feed.** [List dashboard banners](/api-reference/dashboard-banners/list-dashboard-banners)
  returns every banner. [Get the dashboard feed](/api-reference/dashboard-banners/get-the-dashboard-feed) returns only the active ones, and beside them the
  support contact details the dashboard shows.
