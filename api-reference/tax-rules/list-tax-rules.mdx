---
title: "List tax rules"
description: "Returns up to 100 tax rules per call, each with its tax code and tax zones."
openapi: "GET /admin/tax_rules"
---

Returns up to 100 tax rules under `tax_rules`, and the rule count and page position under `pagination`. Each rule has ten fields, including its tax code in `code` and its tax zones in `zones`. To read every rule, step `offset` by the number of rules each page returned until you reach `pagination.total`. `q` and `field_metadata` are accepted and ignored: `q` does not filter the rules.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
