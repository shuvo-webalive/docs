---
title: "Update a tax rule"
description: "Changes a tax rule's `name` or `description` and returns the updated rule."
openapi: "PUT /admin/tax_rules/{tax_rule_id}"
---

Changes the fields you send and returns the whole rule; fields you leave out keep their values. Wrap the fields in `tax_rule`. `name` and `description` are the fields you can change: `code`, `zones`, `decimal_point` and `rounding_type` are accepted and left as they were.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token (New)](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token-new), or from [Get an access token (Legacy)](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
