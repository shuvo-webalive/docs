---
title: "List the states of a country"
description: "Returns the country's states, up to 20 per call, with the country's `id`, `code` and `name`."
openapi: "GET /admin/countries/{country_id}/states"
---

Returns the states of the country in `country_id`, in ascending `id` order, 20 per call unless you send `limit`. The country's `id`, `code` and `name` come in `country`, next to `states` and `pagination`. The query parameters work as they do on [List countries](/api-reference/countries/list-countries), except that a state `code` is one to three letters or digits. A country with no states returns an empty `states` list and a `total` of `0`.

To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
