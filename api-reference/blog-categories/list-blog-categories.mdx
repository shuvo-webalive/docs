---
title: "List blog categories"
description: "Returns up to 20 blog categories, ordered by `name`, with the total and page links."
openapi: "GET /admin/blog_categories"
---

Returns up to 20 blog categories under `blog_categories`, ordered by `name` and ignoring letter case, and `pagination` with the total and the links to the next and previous pages. Send `sort` and `dir` to change the order, and `page` or `offset` to get the next page. Only `limit`, `offset`, `page`, `sort`, `dir` and `field_metadata` are accepted; any other parameter, such as `q` or `name`, is rejected with `400`. After a rejected `limit`, `offset`, `page`, `sort` or `dir`, your next request on the same client returns that same `400`, even when its own parameters are valid.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
