---
title: "Get shopping cart settings"
description: "Returns both shopping cart sections and a `group_meta` block describing each one."
openapi: "GET /admin/settings/page_settings/shopping_cart"
---

Returns every shopping cart setting under `shopping_cart`, with the `cart_page` and `flying_cart` sections in full. Beside it, `group_meta` gives each section's `kind`, whether it is `writable`, and the `href` of its own path. Add `field_metadata=true` to also get a description of every field, keyed by section.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token).</Note>
