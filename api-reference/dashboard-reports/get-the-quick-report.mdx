---
title: "Get the quick report"
description: "Returns the dashboard's KPI figures, order and sales totals, and most viewed items for a period."
openapi: "GET /admin/dashboard/reports/quick"
---

Returns `kpis` (the 27 metrics, or only the ones you list in `kpis`), `orders`, `sales`, `customers`, `products`, `page_views`, `view_report` and 12 `trends`, for the period you send. `view_report` ranks the most viewed pages, or products when you send `entity_name` `product`. The period is `last_30_days` unless you send `duration`, or `start_date` with `end_date`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
