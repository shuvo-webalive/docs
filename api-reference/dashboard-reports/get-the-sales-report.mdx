---
title: "Get the sales report"
description: "Returns sales by day or month for a period, with totals."
openapi: "GET /admin/dashboard/reports/sales"
---

Returns `rows`, one for each day or month in the period that has sales, and `totals`. `group_by` in the response is `day` for a period of about a month or less and `month` for a longer one. Add `breakdown=true` to get orders, shipping, tax, discounts, refunds and the top product on each row and in the totals. Sort with `sort` and `order`. A period with no sales returns `rows` as an empty list.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
