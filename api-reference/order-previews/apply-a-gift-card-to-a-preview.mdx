---
title: "Apply a gift card to a preview"
description: "Applies a gift card to the preview, lowering the amount due."
openapi: "POST /admin/orders/preview/{preview_session_id}/gift-card"
---

Send the card's `code` at the top level of the body. The card pays up to its balance: `discounts.gift_card.applied_amount` is the smaller of the card's balance and `grand_total`, `totals.gift_card_applied` equals it, `grand_total` does not change and `due` is `grand_total` minus `gift_card_applied`. The preview spends nothing from the card. A preview takes one gift card, and the card must belong to the previewed customer. This call moves `session.expires_at` to 30 minutes after the call.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token from [Get an access token (New)](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token-new), or from [Get an access token (Legacy)](/api-reference/authentication/get-an-access-token) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
