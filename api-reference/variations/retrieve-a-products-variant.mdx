---
title: "Retrieve a product's variant"
description: "Returns one variant of the product, with its options, pricing, stock and order quantities."
openapi: "GET /admin/products/{product_id}/variations/{variation_id}"
---

Returns the variant whose `variation_id` you pass, under the `variation` key: its `name` and SKUs, whether it is `active`, its `options` (one for each variation type, with the type's name in `type`), `pricing`, `inventory`, `ordering`, its `transaction_no` and its own `url`. Do not rely on the order of `options`. The variant must belong to the product you pass as `product_id`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
