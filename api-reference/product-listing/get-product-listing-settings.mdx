---
title: "Get product listing settings"
description: "Returns all four product listing sections and a `group_meta` block describing each one."
openapi: "GET /admin/settings/product_listing"
---

Returns every product listing setting under `product_listing`, with all four sections in full: `product_information`, `load`, `sorting` and `sortable_items`. Beside it, `group_meta` holds one `product_listing` entry for the whole area, with its `kind`, whether it is `writable`, and its `href`. No section has a path of its own. Add `field_metadata=true` to also get a description of every field, keyed by section. The response has no `ETag` header.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
