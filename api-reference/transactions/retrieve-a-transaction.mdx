---
title: "Retrieve a transaction"
description: "Returns how many records the number in `transaction_no` is stamped on, for each record type."
openapi: "GET /admin/transactions/{transaction_no}"
---

Returns the number, its `total_records` and `by_type`, at the top level of the body with no wrapping key. `by_type` always has the same 15 rows in the same order, one per record type, each with `type` and `count`. Each row also splits `count` into `created`, `updated` and `trashed`, and has an `items_url`, also when `count` is `0`. To list the records of one type, use [List the records of a transaction](/api-reference/transactions/list-the-records-of-a-transaction).

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
