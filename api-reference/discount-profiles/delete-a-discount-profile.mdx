---
title: "Delete a discount profile"
description: "Deletes a discount profile and its coupon codes permanently, and returns no body."
openapi: "DELETE /admin/discount_profiles/{discount_profile_id}"
---

Deletes the profile whose `id` you pass as `discount_profile_id`, with all its coupon codes, and returns `204` with no body. The profile leaves the listing, and `pagination.total` falls by one. Retrieving, replacing or deleting it again returns `404`, and assigning one of its codes to a customer is rejected with `coupon.not.found.by.code`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
