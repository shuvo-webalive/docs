---
title: "Get a dashboard section"
description: "Returns what one tab of the dashboard's section tabs shows for a period."
openapi: "GET /admin/dashboard/sections/{section}"
---

Returns `section` (the tab you asked for), `period`, and the fields of that tab: order updates and best sellers for `ecommerce`, content to review and top pages for `content`, bookings for `bookings`, enrolments and unpaid orders for `courses`, and comments, drafts and most viewed posts for `blog`. The period is `last_30_days` unless you send `duration`, or `start_date` with `end_date`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
