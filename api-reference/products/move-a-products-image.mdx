---
title: "Move a product's image"
description: "Moves one image of the product to the position you send and returns the image."
openapi: "PATCH /admin/products/{product_id}/media/images/{image_id}"
---

Send `position`, counted from `1`, at the top level of the body with no wrapper. `position` is the only field the call takes: any other body, `alt_text` and a wrapper included, is rejected with `400` and the reason `invalid_body`. The call returns the image as one flat row, not wrapped in `product`. The image at position `1` is `primary`. To order all images at once, use [Set the order of a product's images](/api-reference/products/set-the-order-of-a-products-images).

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. To send a raw JSON body instead of filling in fields, copy the cURL sample and edit its `-d` payload.</Note>
