---
title: "List special products"
description: "Returns one selection of products: on sale, featured, new, top selling, or a customer's last bought or last viewed."
openapi: "GET /admin/products/special"
---

Send `type` to choose the selection: `onSale`, `featured`, `new`, `topSelling`, `lastBought` or `lastViewed`. Each row has the product's name, SKU, image, URL and its price fields, such as `base_price`, `sale_price` and `tax`. Page with `max` (`1` to `100`, default `10`) and `offset`; `pagination.limit` repeats `max`. `lastBought` and `lastViewed` are per customer, so also send `customerId` or `customer_email`. Parameters the endpoint does not know, `page` included, are ignored.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
