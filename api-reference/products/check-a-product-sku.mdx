---
title: "Check a product SKU"
description: "Tells you whether a SKU is free and, when it is not, which product holds it."
openapi: "GET /admin/products/check-sku"
---

Send `sku` to learn whether a product already holds it. The match ignores letter case and also finds a SKU held by a product in the trash and by a variant. A variant's SKU is reported under the variant's own product `id`, not the `id` of the product it belongs to. Send `exclude_product_id` with the `id` of the product you are editing to treat that product's own SKU as free. [Create a product](/api-reference/products/create-a-product) rejects a SKU another product holds with `409`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
