---
title: "Retrieve a product"
description: "Returns the product whose `id` you pass as `product_id`, with all 46 of its fields."
openapi: "GET /admin/products/{product_id}"
---

Returns one product under the `product` key, with its prices, availability, stock, media, tags, reviews and variations. Prices are in `pricing` and the flags in `advanced.product_flags`: flat fields such as `base_price` and `is_featured` are not returned. Send `field_metadata=true` to also get a description of each field. `field_metadata` is the only query parameter accepted: any other, such as `limit` or `fields`, is rejected with `400`. `password` reads `***` when the product has a password and `""` when it has none. A product you deleted is still returned, with `status` set to `trashed`.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
