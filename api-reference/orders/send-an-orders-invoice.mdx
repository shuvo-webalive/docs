---
title: "Send an order's invoice"
description: "Emails an order's invoice to its billing email address."
openapi: "POST /admin/orders/{order_id}/send-invoice"
---

Pass the order's `order_id`. The call needs no body. The response names the address in `sent_to`, the time in `sent_at`, and `template` as `send-invoice`.

**Before you send an invoice, check that the store has an email provider.** Call [Get a settings section](/api-reference/site-settings/get-a-settings-section) with the section `email_settings`: a `service_provider` of `DEFAULT`, as on a new store, means this call answers `409` with the message `No email provider is configured for this store`. To fix it, choose a provider with [Update a settings section](/api-reference/site-settings/update-a-settings-section), for example `{"email_settings": {"service_provider": "SMTP", "smtp": {"host": "smtp.example.com.au", "port": 587, "encryption": "starttls"}}}` with your sender and login details. `encryption` takes `no`, `ssl` or `starttls`. SendGrid takes `sendgrid.api_key`; Gmail and Office 365 connect through the WebCommander admin. A `502` with the message `The invoice email could not be delivered` means the provider is chosen but the email did not go out: check its credentials and the order's billing email address.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
