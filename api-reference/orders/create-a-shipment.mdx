---
title: "Create a shipment"
description: "Ships units of an order's lines and returns the new shipment with its `shipping_id`."
openapi: "POST /admin/orders/{order_id}/shipments"
---

Send `method`, `shipping_date` and `shipment_items` at the top level of the body; `tracking_info` is optional. The response returns `method` as `shipping_method`, `shipping_date` as `date_created` and `tracking_info` as `tracking_number`, which is `null` when you leave `tracking_info` out, and each line shows `shipped_quantity` beside `order_quantity`. The order's `shipping_status` moves to `partial`, and to `shipped` once every unit has shipped, and the first shipment's method becomes `shipping.shipping_type`. On a store whose `update_stock` setting is `after_shipment`, shipping a product that tracks its stock lowers that stock by the units shipped.

**Get the line ids first.** Each `shipment_items` row takes the order line's `order_item_id`, which [Retrieve an order](/api-reference/orders/retrieve-an-order) returns as `order_line_details[].item_id`; any other id is refused with `400` and the message `invalid.order.item`. A shipment lowers stock only when the store's `update_stock` setting, in the `product_inventory` section of [Get a settings section](/api-reference/ecommerce-settings/get-a-settings-section), is `after_shipment`, and only for products whose stock tracking is on: turn it on first with [Change a product's stock](/api-reference/products/change-a-products-stock).
