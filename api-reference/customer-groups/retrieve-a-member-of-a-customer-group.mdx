---
title: "Retrieve a member of a customer group"
description: "Returns one member of a customer group, by the customer's `customer_id`."
openapi: "GET /admin/customer_groups/{customer_group_id}/customers/{customer_id}"
---

Returns the member under `customer`, with the same four fields as the member's entry in the group's `customers` list: `id`, which is the customer's `customer_id`, `first_name`, `last_name` and `email`. Use it to check whether a customer belongs to a group: a customer who is not a member returns `404`. The response has no `ETag` header.

<Note>The SDK samples get their client from credentials your app registers once when it starts, as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#set-up-a-client). To try the call here, click **Try it**, set `store` to your store's host name and paste an access token. Get the token as shown in [Authentication (New)](/authentication#calling-the-api-directly), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy#calling-the-api-directly) if your store signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string.</Note>
