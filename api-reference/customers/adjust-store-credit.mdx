---
title: "Adjust store credit"
description: "Adds to or deducts from a customer's store credit and returns the new balance."
openapi: "POST /admin/customers/{customer_id}/store-credit/adjustments"
---

Adds credit to a customer or deducts it, and records the change in their store credit history. Send `action`, `amount` and an optional `note` at the top level of the JSON body, not inside a `customer` object. `action` is `add` or `deduct` and sets the direction, so `amount` is always a positive number. Save `balance_before` and `balance_after` from the response if you need them, because [List store credit adjustments](/api-reference/customers/list-store-credit-adjustments) does not return them.
