---
title: "Adjust store credit"
description: "Add to or deduct from a customer's store credit."
openapi: "POST /admin/customers/{customer_id}/store-credit/adjustments"
---

The body is the adjustment itself, with no wrapping key. `action` is `add` or `deduct` and carries the direction; `amount` is always positive. The answer carries the adjustment and the balance it left.

<Note>The SDK samples assume a client set up once, as shown in [Authentication](/authentication#set-up-a-client).</Note>
