The Products API has twenty-seven endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/products`.

<Warning>
  **Setting a product's stock unassigns every location you leave out, even one that still holds stock.**
  [Set a product's stock](/api-reference/products/set-a-products-stock) replaces the product's whole set of locations with the
  rows you send in `locations`. Read the rows first with [Retrieve a product's stock](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-products-stock),
  send every row you want to keep, and change only the values you mean to change.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Send product fields at the top level of the body, not inside `product`.** Responses return the
  product inside `product`, but a create or `PUT` body wrapped in `product` is rejected with `400` and
  the message `product: is not a recognised product field`. A `PATCH` body wrapped in `product` is
  rejected with `400` in a different error shape, with `code` set to `validation_error` and the rejected
  fields in `errors`. Send the stock body's `note` and `locations` at the top level too.
  [Import a product's reviews](/api-reference/products/import-a-products-reviews) is the exception: wrap its reviews in `product`.
- **`PUT` and `PATCH` both change only the fields you send, at every level.** `{"advanced": {"advance_information": {"mpn": "MPN-0001"}}}`
  changes `mpn` and keeps the other eight fields of `advance_information`. A body that changes nothing,
  `{}` included, returns `200` and leaves `version` and the `ETag` header as they were.
- **Deleting a product moves it to the trash.** [Delete a product](/api-reference/products/delete-a-product) returns `204`, but
  [Retrieve a product](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-product) still returns the product, with `status` set to `trashed`.
  Every write to a product in the trash, including `PUT`, `PATCH` and importing reviews, is rejected with
  `409`, and deleting it again returns `404` with the message `Product already in trash`.
- **Tag a create with a transaction number in the `X-Transaction-No` header or the body.** [Create a product](/api-reference/products/create-a-product),
  [Import a product's reviews](/api-reference/products/import-a-products-reviews) and [Import a product's variants](/api-reference/variations/import-a-products-variants)
  take an optional `X-Transaction-No` header; in the SDKs, pass `transaction_no`. You can send
  `transaction_no` in the body instead. When you send both with different values, the header's value is
  stored and the body's is lost, with no error. On `PUT` and `PATCH` the header is ignored, and a body
  `transaction_no` is stored only on a product that has none: a product that has one keeps it.
- **Some fields cannot be written.** A changed `id`, `created_at`, `created_on`, `custom_class`,
  `sold_out`, `sold_out_label`, `length`, `width`, `height`, `weight`, `featured`, `new`, `model`, `is_draft`,
  `has_variations`, `urls` or `created_by` is rejected with `400` and the reason `read_only_field`; the value
  you read is accepted. `status` and `reviews` are rejected with the reason `unknown_field`, so remove them
  before you send back a product you read. Write the dimensions in `product_properties`, and `mpn`, `upc`,
  `barcode` and `hs_code` in `advanced.advance_information`. `field_metadata` lists these fields as writable
  at the top level, but they are rejected there.
- **Setting stock changes the product too.** `available_stock` is the sum of the rows' `stock`, and
  `product_pricing_and_stock.low_level_stock` takes the `low_stock_level` of the product's default
  location. The product's `version`, `updated_at` and `updated_by` change when the stock changes.
  `product_pricing_and_stock.locations` on [Retrieve a product](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-product) holds the same rows as
  [Retrieve a product's stock](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-products-stock).
- **The first location you assign to a product is missing from the response.** On a product with no
  location yet, [Set a product's stock](/api-reference/products/set-a-products-stock) returns `locations: []` and stores the row.
  Call [Retrieve a product's stock](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-products-stock) to see it. A product that already has a location
  returns its rows at once.
- **Find a `product_id` in the product list, a create response or an inventory location's stock.**
  [List products](/api-reference/products/list-products) returns every product with its `id`. [Create a product](/api-reference/products/create-a-product) returns the new product's `id` as
  `product.id`. [List stock at an inventory location](/api-reference/inventory-locations/list-stock-at-an-inventory-location)
  returns the `id` of each product the location holds, as `items[].product.id`.
- **A `PUT` must include ten fields.** [Update a product](/api-reference/products/update-a-product) rejects a body that leaves out `name`, `sku`,
  `base_price`, `product_type`, `available`, `is_on_sale`, `available_on_date_range`, `available_on_weekdays`,
  `available_for` or `selected_customers` with `400` and the reason `required`. Every other field you leave out
  keeps its value. [Patch a product](/api-reference/products/patch-a-product) has no required fields.
- **A product returns its prices, flags and availability inside blocks, not as flat fields.** A product returns 46 fields, among them `pricing`, `inventory`, `ordering`,
  `restrictions`, `tax`, `shipping`, `available_for_purchase`, `media`, `categories`, `up_sell_products`, `summary`, `description`, `virtual`, `product_url`, `updated_by` and `links`,
  and `advanced` holds `product_flags`, `product_dimension`, `product_identifiers` and `webtool`. Read the base price from `pricing.base_price` and the featured flag from
  `advanced.product_flags.featured.enabled`: the flat fields `base_price`, `is_featured` and the others that moved into blocks are not returned. A create and a `PATCH` still accept the flat
  names, so `{"base_price": 3.5}` changes `pricing.base_price`.
  These block fields are read-only: `product_url`, `links`, `restrictions.enabled`, `restrictions.display_only`,
  `restrictions.price.enabled`, `restrictions.purchase.enabled`, `shipping.enabled`, the two units in
  `advanced.product_dimension`, `advanced.product_flags.sold_out` and `advanced.webtool`. A value you send in
  `updated_by`, `restrictions.price.customers` or `restrictions.purchase.customers` returns `200` and is not kept.
- **Some block fields behave differently from the flat ones.** `up_sell_products` takes `{"id": ...}` records: a list
  of bare ids returns `200` and leaves `up_sell_products` empty. `categories` takes category ids or
  `{"id": ...}` records, and `[]` changes nothing. `media` names files the product already holds. `shipping.profile`
  (an id or `{"id": ...}`) is kept by an update and dropped by a create. `tax.custom_tax_profile` takes the id of a
  custom tax profile. While `available_for_purchase.enabled` is `false`, a value you write in its `who_can_view`,
  `schedule` or `password_protection` returns `200` and is not kept.
- **`selected_customers` and `restrict_for_selected_customers` are objects.** Each reads
  `{"customers": [], "groups": []}` when nobody is named. To write `selected_customers`, send the customer ids in
  `customers` and the customer group ids in `groups`; a group sent as `{"id": ...}` is rejected with `400`.
  `restrict_for_selected_customers` is read-only.
- **Upload images as JSON with the file base64-encoded.** [Upload images to a product](/api-reference/products/upload-images-to-a-product) and [Add an image to a product's media](/api-reference/products/add-an-image-to-a-products-media) reject a multipart body
  with `415`, the message `Content-Type must be application/json` and `details[0].field` set to `Content-Type`. Send the file's bytes as base64 text. Only images are accepted: a `type` of `videos` or
  `specification_file` on [Add an image to a product's media](/api-reference/products/add-an-image-to-a-products-media) is rejected with `400` and the reason `not_supported`. The store keeps the file under your `filename` with a UUID and `_`
  in front, such as `<uuid>_front.png`, so compare the end of the name.
- **The two upload calls take different bodies and return different shapes.** [Upload images to a product](/api-reference/products/upload-images-to-a-product) takes `{"product": {"image_and_video": {"images": [{"filename": ..., "base64": ...}]}}}`,
  stores one or more files, and returns `201` with only the files it just stored in `product.images`, and no `Location` header. [Add an image to a product's media](/api-reference/products/add-an-image-to-a-products-media) takes one file as
  `{"type": "images", "filename": ..., "content": ...}` at the top level, where `content` is base64 text or a `data:image/png;base64,...` address, and returns `201` with a `Location` header and the stored image
  as one flat row: `id`, `filename`, `position`, `primary`, `alt_text`, `url` and `thumbnail_url`. `position` is optional. A field named `base64` on that call is rejected with `400` and the reason `unknown_field`.
  Pass the image's `id` as `image_id` to [Delete a product's image](/api-reference/products/delete-a-products-image). Deleting it again returns `404`.
- **Delete several products moves them to the trash and returns `200` even when some fail.** Send `{"ids": [...]}` to [Delete several products](/api-reference/products/delete-several-products); `product_ids` is rejected with `400` and the reason `required`,
  and so is an empty list. `deleted` counts the products moved to the trash, `failed` counts the others, and `errors` lists each failure with its `id` and a `reason` such as `not_found`. A product in the trash
  still holds its SKU, which [Check a product SKU](/api-reference/products/check-a-product-sku) reports with `status` set to `trashed`, and neither [Count products](/api-reference/products/count-products) nor [List products](/api-reference/products/list-products) includes it.
- **Count products takes the list's filters, but not its paging or sorting.** [Count products](/api-reference/products/count-products) returns `total` and `count`, which hold the same number, for the same filters as [List products](/api-reference/products/list-products),
  so the count equals `pagination.total` of the list with the same filters. `limit`, `offset` and `sortBy` are rejected with `400` and the reason `unknown_parameter`. `status` takes `active`, `inactive` or `draft`,
  and `product_type` takes `physical`, `downloadable`, `gift_card` or `bundle`; any other value is rejected with `400` and the reason `invalid_enum`. `category` is accepted and filters nothing. A list or a count without `status` includes draft products.
- **List special products needs `type`, and two types need a customer.** Send `type` set to `onSale`, `featured`, `new`, `topSelling`, `lastBought` or `lastViewed`, in that letter case, to [List special products](/api-reference/products/list-special-products).
  `max` is `1` to `100` and defaults to `10`, and `offset` is `0` or more. `lastBought` and `lastViewed` are per customer: send `customerId` (the customer's `customer_id` or `internal_id`) or `customer_email`.
  `customer_id` is not read, and a `customerId` no customer has is rejected with `400` and the reason `unknown_reference`. A per-customer list with no products also returns `"status": "success"`.
- **Check a product SKU also sees trashed products and variants.** [Check a product SKU](/api-reference/products/check-a-product-sku) ignores letter case. A SKU held by a variant is reported under the variant's own product `id`, not the `id` of the parent
  product, and a SKU held by a product in the trash is reported with `status` set to `trashed`. Send `exclude_product_id` with the `id` of the product you are editing to treat its own SKU as free.
- **A review's `status` is `pending` or `approved`, and the review list pages.** [Patch a product's review](/api-reference/products/patch-a-products-review) takes `status`, `rating` and `comment`, and a `status` of `rejected` is rejected with `400` and the reason `invalid_enum`.
  [List a product's reviews](/api-reference/products/list-a-products-reviews) takes `limit` (`1` to `100`, default `20`) and `offset` and returns a `pagination` block. It ignores `status` and `page`: a list sent with `status=pending` still returns approved reviews.
  [Retrieve a product's review](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-products-review) returns `404` with the message `Review not found` when no review has that `review_id`, and `400` when the review belongs to another product.
- **Updating a product's inventory sets the stock figure, and a variant keeps its own.** [Update a product's inventory](/api-reference/products/update-a-products-inventory) takes `track_inventory`, `available_stock` and `low_level_stock` inside
  `product.product_pricing_and_stock` and returns `201`. Read the new stock with [Retrieve a product's stock](/api-reference/products/retrieve-a-products-stock). The stock of a product's variants does not change. The call takes the optional `X-Transaction-No` header:
  the number is stored when the product has none, but the response to that first write shows the earlier value, `null`.
- **Set the order of all images, or move one.** [Set the order of a product's images](/api-reference/products/set-the-order-of-a-products-images) takes `{"type": "images", "ids": [...]}` and must name every image of the product exactly once, otherwise it is rejected with `400`
  and the reason `invalid_set`. The first `id` becomes the `primary` image. [Move a product's image](/api-reference/products/move-a-products-image) takes only `{"position": n}`, counted from `1`; any other body, `alt_text` and a wrapper included, is rejected with `400` and the reason `invalid_body`.
  Both send their fields at the top level of the body, with no wrapper.
- **Change stock with a `PATCH`, and expect `null` transaction numbers in the history.** [Change a product's stock](/api-reference/products/change-a-products-stock) takes `tracking`, `locations`, `note` and `transaction_no` at the top level of the body, and at least one of them.
  A wrapper, or `available_stock` outside `tracking`, is rejected with `400` and the reason `unknown_field`. It returns `adjustment` as `null` when the stock figure did not change, and the adjustment's `note` is `via API` unless you send one.
  [List a product's stock adjustments](/api-reference/products/list-a-products-stock-adjustments) returns every row with `transaction_no` set to `null`, also for a change you sent with a `transaction_no`, so do not look a change up by its transaction number.
