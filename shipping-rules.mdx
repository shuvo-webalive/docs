The Shipping rules API has ten endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/shipping_rules`.

<Warning>
  **A shipping rule is deleted permanently, and a rule that is on a shipping profile cannot be deleted.**
  [Delete a shipping rule](/api-reference/shipping-rules/delete-a-shipping-rule) on a rule that a profile uses is rejected with `409`, and the
  response names the profile. The rule's `profiles` list shows which profiles use it. Take the rule off those
  profiles first. Deleting a rule keeps its shipping rate and its zones.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication (New)](/authentication), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy) if your store
signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Create, replace and update wrap the rule in `shipping_rule`.** Send `{"shipping_rule": {...}}`. A body
  without the wrapper is rejected with `400`. A field the rule does not have is rejected with `400` and the
  reason `unknown_field`, and an `id` in the body with `400` and the reason `not_allowed`. Responses return
  the rule inside `shipping_rule`.
- **A rule has a shipping class, a shipping rate and zones, and you send each one by its `id`.**
  `shipping_class` and `shipping_rate` take an `id`, either bare as `2` or as `{"id": 2}`, or `null`.
  `zones` takes the whole list of tax zone `id` values, bare or as `{"id": ...}`, each zone once. An
  unknown `id`, `0`, a decimal such as `1.5`, text, or a repeated zone is rejected with `400`. A class,
  rate or zone you leave out reads `null`, `null` and `[]`.
- **`PUT` replaces a rule, and `PATCH` changes only the fields you send.**
  [Replace a shipping rule](/api-reference/shipping-rules/replace-a-shipping-rule) needs `name`, `description`, `shipping_class`,
  `shipping_rate` and `zones`, and names each missing field in the `400` response with the reason
  `required`. The first three take `null`, and `zones` takes `[]`. [Update a shipping rule](/api-reference/shipping-rules/update-a-shipping-rule)
  changes only what you send: `zones` is the full new list, and a `shipping_rate` or `shipping_class` of
  `null` takes it off the rule. The rate and the zones themselves stay in the store.
- **Send `profile_id` on a create to put the new rule on a shipping profile.** The profile then lists the
  rule in `shipping_rules`. A `profile_id` that no profile has is rejected with `400`.
- **Filters on a listing take `id` values, and each rule is listed once.** `shipping_class_id` and
  `zone_id` keep the rules that have that class or zone. A class or zone `id` that no record has is
  rejected with `400` and the reason `invalid_reference`. `method` and `handling_cost` match the
  rule's shipping rate.
- **A listing returns 20 rules per call unless you send `limit`, and the newest rule comes first.** `limit`
  takes `1` to `100`, and `offset` skips rules. To get more, send a higher `offset` while
  `pagination.has_next` is `true`. A query parameter the listing does not know is ignored.
