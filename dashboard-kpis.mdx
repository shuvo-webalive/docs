The Dashboard KPIs API has three endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/dashboard`.

<Warning>
  **[Update the KPI selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/update-the-kpi-selection) changes the tiles on the dashboard of every admin.**
  It writes the KPI tiles of the store's default dashboard layout, so the change shows at once for everyone,
  and the layout's `updated_at` moves. Before you call it, read the keys in `selected` from
  [List KPI options](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/list-kpi-options), so you can send them back to restore the tiles.
</Warning>

## At a glance

| Endpoint | Method | Path | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [List KPI options](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/list-kpi-options) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/dashboard/kpi_options` | Returns every metric a KPI tile can show, by group, and the keys now selected. |
| [Get the KPI selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/get-the-kpi-selection) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#0f7b6c"}}>GET</span> | `…/dashboard/kpi_selection` | Returns the KPI tiles the dashboard shows, with their figures for a period. |
| [Update the KPI selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/update-the-kpi-selection) | <span style={{fontFamily: "var(--font-mono, monospace)", fontSize: "0.72rem", fontWeight: 700, letterSpacing: "0.02em", color: "#b45309"}}>PUT</span> | `…/dashboard/kpi_selection` | Sets the 2 to 5 KPI tiles the dashboard shows, for every admin, and returns them. |

## Before you call

Register your credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication (New)](/authentication), or in [Authentication (Legacy)](/authentication-legacy) if your store
signs in with a redirect URI and an auth string. Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Select metrics by `key`, not by `code`.** Each metric has a `key` and a `code`. For most metrics they are
  the same. For some they differ, such as `key` `aov` with `code` `average_order_value`. [Update the KPI
  selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/update-the-kpi-selection) takes the `key`.
- **The dashboard shows 2 to 5 tiles.** `min` and `max` in [List KPI options](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/list-kpi-options) give the
  limits. The store has 27 metrics in five groups: `ecommerce`, `content`, `blog`, `bookings` and `courses`.
- **The figures follow the period you send.** [Get the KPI selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/get-the-kpi-selection) and the update
  take `duration` (the default is `last_30_days`) or `start_date` with `end_date` as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Each tile
  has `value` and `previous_value`, the figure for the period before it, and `change_percent`, which is `null`
  when `previous_value` is `0`. A bad value, or one date without the other, is rejected with `400`.
- **The update sends the full list, and the tiles follow your order.** The response has the same fields as
  [Get the KPI selection](/api-reference/dashboard-kpis/get-the-kpi-selection), with the tiles in the order you sent and `position` from `1`.
  A body with a field other than `metrics` is not an error: the other fields are ignored.
