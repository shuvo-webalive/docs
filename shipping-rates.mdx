The Shipping rates API has ten endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/shipping_rates`.

<Warning>
  **A shipping rate is deleted permanently, and a rate that a shipping rule uses cannot be deleted.**
  [Delete a shipping rate](/api-reference/shipping-rates/delete-a-shipping-rate) on a rate that a rule uses is rejected with `409`, and the
  response names the rule. The rate's `shipping_rules` list shows which rules use it. Delete those rules,
  or take the rate off them, first.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Create, replace and update wrap the rate in `shipping_rate`.** Send `{"shipping_rate": {...}}`. A body
  without the wrapper is rejected with `400`, the reason `required` and the message
  `shipping_rate: must be an object`. A field the rate does not have is rejected with `400` and the reason
  `unknown_field`, and an `id` in the body with `400` and the reason `not_allowed`. Responses return the
  rate inside `shipping_rate`.
- **`method` decides which fields a rate takes.** `method` is `flat_rate`, `free_shipping`, `ship_by_price`,
  `ship_by_quantity`, `ship_by_weight`, `ship_by_variation` or `shipping_variant`, and it is required. A
  `flat_rate` needs `shipping_cost`, and takes `handling_cost` and `additional_rule`. The four `ship_by_...`
  methods take their costs per row in `conditions`, and a `shipping_cost` at the top level of the rate is
  rejected with `400` and the reason `not_allowed`. `free_shipping` and `shipping_variant` need no cost.
  A cost is `{"amount": 10, "type": "fixed"}`, and `type` is `fixed` or `percent`.
- **A `conditions` row depends on the method.** A `ship_by_price` or `ship_by_quantity` row has `from`, `to`,
  `shipping_cost` and `handling_cost`. A `ship_by_weight` row also has `packet_weight`. A
  `ship_by_variation` row has `variation_combination`, a list of `{"id": ...}` with a variation's `id`,
  instead of `from` and `to`. Rows whose `from` and `to` overlap are rejected with `400` and the reason
  `overlapping_range`.
- **`free_shipping` always saves `calculate_for_entire_order` as `false`.** The store ignores a `true` you
  send. `price_entered_with_tax` keeps the value you send, and the rate's `shipping_cost` and
  `additional_rule` read `null`.
- **`PUT` replaces a rate, and `PATCH` changes only the fields you send.**
  [Replace a shipping rate](/api-reference/shipping-rates/replace-a-shipping-rate) needs `name`, `method`, `calculate_for_entire_order` and
  `price_entered_with_tax`, the costs the method uses, `handling_cost` and `additional_rule`, and names each
  missing field in the `400` response with the reason `required`. `handling_cost` and `additional_rule`
  take `null`. To change one field, use [Update a shipping rate](/api-reference/shipping-rates/update-a-shipping-rate).
- **A listing returns 20 rates per call unless you send `limit`, and the newest rate comes first.** `limit`
  takes `1` to `100`, and `offset` skips rates. To get more, send a higher `offset` while
  `pagination.has_next` is `true`. A query parameter the listing does not know is ignored.
- **A rate created a long time ago can return a `method` other than the seven names.** For example
  `FLAT_RATE` or `[sbq, sbq]`, with `shipping_cost` `null` and no rows in `conditions`. Treat any other
  value as unknown, and send only the seven names on a write.
