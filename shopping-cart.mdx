The Shopping cart API has five endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/settings/page_settings/shopping_cart`.

<Warning>
  Every write body must be wrapped in a key, and the key depends on the path. On `/shopping_cart`,
  wrap the fields in `shopping_cart` and then in the section name. On `/shopping_cart/{section}`,
  wrap them in the section's own name only, such as `cart_page`: a body wrapped in
  `shopping_cart`, or not wrapped at all, is rejected with `400 invalid_request`.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **The settings are split into two sections.** `cart_page` controls the cart page: product
  images, the empty cart button and the Save cart button. `flying_cart` turns the slide-out flying
  cart on or off and controls its empty cart button and Save cart action. Read or write both
  through `/shopping_cart`, or one through `/shopping_cart/{section}`. An unknown section returns
  `404 not_found`.
- **`PATCH` is the only way to write.** `PUT`, `POST` and `DELETE` are rejected on `/shopping_cart`
  and on both section paths with `405 method_not_allowed` and an `Allow: GET, HEAD, PATCH, OPTIONS`
  header. On `/shopping_cart`, only the fields you send change, and one body can change both
  sections.
- **Add `field_metadata=true` to a read to get a description of every field.** The settings are
  still returned with it. Each entry gives the field's `ui_label`, `description`, `type` and
  `default`, and every field is a `boolean`. The two `save_cart` fields also have
  `plugin: "save-cart"`, and `flying_cart.enable_clear_cart` and `flying_cart.save_cart` have
  `requires: "enabled"`. Setting `flying_cart.enabled` to `false` leaves those two fields' stored
  values as they are. On `/shopping_cart` the block is keyed by section; on a section it is keyed
  by field name.
- **All shopping cart writes share one rate limit.** If you send eleven writes within about 1.3
  seconds, the eleventh is rejected with `429` and a `Retry-After` header that counts down the time
  left before writes are accepted again. Until then, writes to either section and to
  `/shopping_cart` are all rejected, while reads still return `200`. A retry before then counts as
  another write, so wait until `Retry-After` has passed before you write again.
- **After a rejected write, the next write on the same client returns that rejection again.** It
  comes back under a new `request_id`, even when the second write is correct and goes to the other
  section. The write after that succeeds.
