The Payment gateways API has eight endpoints, and you can call every one from all seven SDKs. Every path starts with `/api/v4/admin/payment_gateways`.

<Warning>
  **You cannot send a retrieved gateway back unchanged.** A `PUT` or `PATCH` with the body from
  [Retrieve a payment gateway](/api-reference/payment-gateways/retrieve-a-payment-gateway) is rejected with `400`: `code` and `promotional`
  are read-only, `surcharge: null` is rejected with `must be an object`, and empty `visibility.customers`
  and `visibility.customer_groups` lists are rejected under `type` `everyone`. Send only the fields
  you want to change.
</Warning>

## Before you call

Register your store's credentials once when your app starts, as shown in
[Authentication](/authentication). Every SDK sample on these pages then gets its client in one line.
With cURL, send the access token in an `Authorization` header: `Authorization: Bearer $ACCESS_TOKEN`.

## Things to know

- **Send the fields at the top level of the body, not inside `payment_gateway`.** Both writes take
  the bare record. A body wrapped in `payment_gateway`, or any field that is not a gateway field, is
  rejected with `400`, the reason `unknown_field` and the message `is not a payment gateway field`.
  Responses return the gateway inside `payment_gateway`.
- **`PUT` and `PATCH` both change only the fields you send.** `PUT` does not replace the gateway: a
  field you leave out keeps its value, as it does with `PATCH`. `allowed_for` merges key by key.
  An empty body `{}` is rejected with `400` and the message `request body is empty`.
- **You cannot create or delete a gateway.** The gateways already exist in your store. `POST` and
  `DELETE` on a gateway, `POST` and `PUT` on the list, and `PATCH` on `/count` are rejected with
  `405 method_not_allowed`. `OPTIONS` returns `204` with an `Allow` header: `GET, HEAD, OPTIONS` on
  the list and on `/count`, and `GET, HEAD, PUT, PATCH, OPTIONS` on a gateway.
- **A listing returns 20 gateways per call unless you send `limit`.** `limit` takes `1` to `100`;
  `0` or a value above `100` is rejected with `400` and the reason `out_of_range`. To get the next
  page, raise `offset` by `limit` while `pagination.has_next` is `true`. A page past the end returns
  no gateways, and its `has_previous` is `false` even though `offset` is above `0`.
- **`settings` holds different fields for each gateway, and only a single-gateway read or a write returns it.**
  Listing rows have no `settings`. Offline gateways return `{}`. A stored secret, such as an API key
  or a client secret, reads `********`. No path returns an `ETag` header.
- **Errors have one shape.** The body is an `error` object with `code`, `message`, `details` and
  `request_id`. Each `details` entry names the rejected `field` and gives its `code` and `message`.
  The entry's `message` is `null` when `limit` is `0` or not a whole number, or `offset` is negative or
  not a whole number; the top-level `message` then gives the reason.
  A read-only field such as `code` gets two entries: `read_only` and `unknown_field`.
